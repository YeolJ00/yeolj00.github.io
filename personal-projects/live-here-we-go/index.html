Set up once

Save this as a bookmark named Here We Go.

On a computer, drag → ⚡ Here We Go  to your bookmarks bar (skips the steps below).

📱 Phone (Chrome, Android)

  1. Tap Copy code above.
  2. Bookmark any page (Chrome menu → ☆).
  3. Open menu → Bookmarks, edit that bookmark: name it Here We Go and paste the code as the address.

💻 Computer

  1. Show the bookmarks bar (Ctrl/⌘+Shift+B).
  2. Drag the ⚡ Here We Go chip above onto it.
  3. That's it. No copy/paste needed.

Every match

Be logged in and watching the match in the same browser.

  1. Open the match on coupangplay.com. On a phone, turn on menu → Desktop site first so it doesn't bounce you to the app.
  2. Run Here We Go. On a phone: tap the address bar, type here we go, tap the suggestion. On a computer: click the bookmark. A ⚡ panel appears.
  3. Pick a quality. The panel shows a 🎬 Pick a quality row (360p → 4K). Tap one and coupang_stream.m3u8 downloads.
  4. Open it in VLC. 🎉 Tap ↺ Other quality anytime to grab a different one.

Not every match can be grabbed. If Here We Go can't pick one up, it'll tell you. Premier League matches work great.

How to find the two URLs (computer)
  1. Open the match on coupangplay.com and start playing.
  2. Press F12 → Network tab.
  3. Type m3u8 in the filter box.
  4. Copy the two playlist URLs (one is video, one is audio) and paste them above.

What's actually happening

Coupang Play never hands you a video file. When you watch a match, your browser downloads it in small chunks, following a playlist, a text file ending in .m3u8 that lists where the video and audio pieces live. That playlist address is the one thing VLC needs, and it's the one thing Coupang keeps out of sight.

On a computer you can dig it out of DevTools. On a phone there are no DevTools at all. Here We Go handles both by reading the playlist the page just loaded, then saving a tidy copy for VLC at the quality you pick.

✗ A normal website can only see its own network activity, never another site's. Browsers enforce that wall on purpose. Otherwise, any page could spy on your email or bank tab. So a Here We Go website sitting in its own tab is blind to the Coupang tab beside it.

A browser extension could also do it, but Chrome on phones doesn't allow extensions, so the bookmark is the one method that works everywhere.

Questions

Why won't some matches grab?
Not every broadcast is served in a form Here We Go can pick up. When that happens it tells you instead of saving something that won't play. Premier League matches work reliably.
Which quality should I pick?
Lower is lighter and smoother on a weak connection. 1080p is a good default; 4K is heavy. You choose per match, and can grab a different one anytime.
What do I open the file with?
VLC on desktop or mobile. Infuse (iOS) and MX Player (Android) work too. Anything that plays HLS.
Does it need my Coupang login?
Yes. You must be logged in and playing the match in the same browser. Here We Go only reads what your session already loaded; it never sees your password.
