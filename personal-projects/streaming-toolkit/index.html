🚀RocketMerge

📋 How to use:

  1. Paste your two stream URLs in the boxes above
  2. Click "Create Stream" to process
  3. Download the generated playlist file
  4. Open the file with a video player:
    • Desktop: VLC, MPV, or any HLS-compatible player
    • Mobile: VLC for Mobile, Infuse (iOS), MX Player (Android)
    • TV: Plex, Kodi, or smart TV built-in players
